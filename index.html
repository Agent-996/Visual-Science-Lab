Visual Science Lab Labs

Science practicals you can do in your browser

Set up the equipment, take your own readings and work out the results, one step at a time. Made for GCSE students (ages 14–16) and the parents helping them revise.

  1. Follow the methodEach step says what to do and why it matters.
  2. Use the equipmentFill a burette, hang masses, weigh samples, and read the scales yourself.
  3. Analyse your resultsFill in a results table, plot a graph and check each calculation.
